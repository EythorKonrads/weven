Questions, answered

Does Weven move my money?

No. Weven keeps track of who owes what and shows your friend's payment details. You pay with MobilePay, your bank or Revolut like you normally would.

Do my friends need the app?

They'll need a free account to join a group, on the web or in the app.

Can we mix currencies on a trip?

Yes. Add each expense in the currency you paid in and Weven converts it to the group's currency at the live rate.

Can I cancel Plus anytime?

Yes, cancel whenever you like. Your groups and history stay on the free plan.
